ABOUT

Welcome to my digital academic haven—a space where curiosity converges with experience. Having completed my Master’s in Advanced Computing at Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, with a dissertation focused on Machine Learning, I am now eager to embark on a career in Artificial Intelligence. Here, I share the insights, projects, and discoveries that have shaped my journey, from foundational studies and technical triumphs to creative problem-solving in the rapidly evolving AI landscape. What began as a simple dream with a laptop and a passion has grown into a dedicated interest in Data Science and a commitment to inspiring others who share this excitement for technology. Through this platform, I hope to document my growth and foster a vibrant community of learning, collaboration, and innovation—contributing to the future of AI.
